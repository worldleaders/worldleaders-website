Parent Guides

Practical guidance for real life

Simple strategies you can use today — built around calm, connection, boundaries, and consistent practice.

Big emotions

How to respond to meltdowns, anger, tears, and overwhelm with calm structure.

Try: Name the feeling → validate → set boundary → offer 2 choices.

Positive discipline

Clear expectations, consistent consequences, and teaching the skill — without shame.

Try: “I can’t let you…” + “Here’s what you can do instead…”

Routines & responsibility

Simple routines that build independence and cooperation.

Try: Morning/bedtime checklists + small chores.

Quick scripts (copy/paste)

When a child refuses: “I hear you don’t want to. The rule is ____. You can choose A or B.”

When a child hits: “I won’t let you hit. Hands are for helping. Let’s take a break and try again.”

When a child lies: “Thank you for telling me now. In our family, we tell the truth even when it’s hard.”
