Try this at home

Small steps. Big encouragement.

Gentle, respectful strategies you can start today — no special equipment, no pressure.

🎯

Attention & focus

Short, clear instructions with predictable routines and movement breaks.

  • One-step directions
  • Timers & visual reminders
  • “First–then” language
  • Reward effort, not just results
🫒

Anxiety & big feelings

Teach calming skills before stressful moments, and reduce uncertainty ahead of time.

  • Breathing & body scans
  • A “what happens next” plan
  • A safe coping corner
  • Offer small, real choices
🧺

Sensory needs

Some children need sensory input to feel regulated, calm, and ready to focus.

  • Heavy-work play
  • Fidgets & quiet headphones
  • Planned sensory breaks
  • Watch for what soothes
