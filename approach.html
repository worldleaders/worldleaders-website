Our Approach

Clear, calm steps you can use at home or in the classroom. We focus on teaching skills — not blaming children.

1

Observe first

Behavior is communication. Look for needs, stress, or missing skills.

Try: “What is this moment telling me?”
2

Adjust the environment

Small changes reduce friction and increase success.

Try: visuals, quieter space, movement breaks
3

Teach the skill

Children need practice: waiting, transitions, asking for help.

Try: practice during calm moments
4

Use calm boundaries

Limits create safety when they’re steady and respectful.

Try: short words, consistent actions
5

Encourage progress

Notice effort and strategies — not perfection.

Try: “You kept trying. That was hard.”
6

Partner with families

Children thrive when home and school are aligned.

Try: one shared routine across settings
